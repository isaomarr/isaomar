About Isa

I'm a GIS Specialist and Remote Sensing Analyst turned entrepreneur. What began as a technical fascination with spatial data grew into GeoStack, a geospatial technology company — and from there into GeoStack Academy, training the next generation of geospatial professionals, and END Group, a multi-sector holding company spanning education, construction, architecture, and engineering.

Across all three companies, my focus stays the same: build systems and teams that turn specialized expertise into lasting, real-world value.
